Dynamics Days is an international meeting focused on nonlinear dynamics, chaos, and the many ways these ideas appear in physics, biology, engineering, and beyond.

The 2027 edition will bring together speakers, students, and visitors for a collaborative program set in the beautiful coastal setting of La Jolla.

### What to expect

- Research talks across multiple themes
- Daily program sessions and networking time
- A welcoming environment for early-career researchers
